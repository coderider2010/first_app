# Project Hub: the answer

**Status:** APPROVED by the Partner, 2026-10-11 (checkpoint 2) · **As of:** 2026-10-11 ·
**Price:** $228.30 (NYSE close, 2026-10-09)

## What is HubSpot worth per share?

> **About $150 to $345 per share, depending on one question: will HubSpot ever turn
> revenue into cash the way mature software companies do? Today's $228 prices in roughly
> a 40% chance that it will.**

| View | Method | Value per share | Module |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Squeezed:** AI pressure keeps margins at ~12% after SBC | DCF | **$149** | 03 |
| Peers, on cash flow (EV / FCF after SBC) | Trading multiples | $161 | 04 |
| **Today's price** | Market | **$228** | — |
| **Grows up:** margins rise to 25%, the low end of peers | DCF | **$344** | 03 |
| Peers, on revenue (EV / revenue); $324 without the two richest peers | Trading multiples | $438 | 04 |

Two independent methods agree on the crux. The DCF stories and the peer comparison were
built without seeing each other, and both place the value on the same hinge: cash
conversion. The peer cash view ($161) sits next to Squeezed ($149); the revenue view,
which assumes peer-like economics, sits above Grows up.

## The assumptions that move the value most

1. **Long-run free cash flow margin after SBC.** Swapping only the margin between the two
   stories moves value by $116–156 per share; swapping growth moves it by $39–79. HubSpot
   converts 4.2% of revenue to FCF after SBC today; peers' median is about 15%. The gap
   is mostly cash generation *before* SBC (19% vs 31%), not SBC itself.
2. **The discount rate.** 10.5% (CAPM: 5.24% Treasury, beta 1.25, 4.20% premium); ±1 point
   moves each story's value substantially (see Module 03's sensitivity table).
3. **The growth path.** The market already accepts ~9% a year for ten years (Module 02),
   consistent with guidance fading from 14%. Growth matters less than margin.

## The working answer, tested

The working answer was: "the price assumes fast growth and rising margins." All three
valuation modules point against it. **The price assumes moderate growth and doubts the
margin.** The Partner kept the hypothesis for Module 3 to test; it did not survive.

## What would tell us which story is true

- **Q3 2026 results (early November):** net revenue retention, net customer additions
  (6,988 in Q2 vs 9,724 a year earlier), and gross margin against AI costs.
- **Whether SBC and cash costs fall as a share of revenue,** per the Analyst Day 2030
  targets (unverified; secondary source only).

## Caveats

- Peer comparison confidence is low (growth-adjusted fit r² 0.05); its top end leans on
  two richly valued peers.
- The share price moved 15% in two weeks; refresh before use.
- Unverified: earnings-call quotes (third-party transcript) and the Analyst Day targets;
  neither feeds a number in the DCF.
