# Brief 01: Company fact pack

**Engagement:** Project Hub (what is HubSpot worth per share?) · **From:** EM
**Skill:** `company-profile` (stub: flag it, then follow the charter)

## Question
What are HubSpot's actual reported numbers? Cover the last three fiscal years plus the
latest reported quarter and the trailing twelve months (TTM).

## Why it matters
Every later module uses these numbers. One wrong figure here spreads into the share
price analysis, the DCF and the peer comparison.

## What to collect
- Revenue: total, subscription and professional services, plus year-over-year growth
- Gross margin, and operating margin on both GAAP and non-GAAP bases
- Stock-based compensation
- Operating cash flow, capital expenditure and free cash flow
- Cash, investments and debt (including convertible notes)
- Diluted share count, and basic shares outstanding at the latest date
- Customers, average subscription revenue per customer, and net revenue retention
- Management's guidance for the current year, if published

## Depth
Exact, as reported. No estimates in this module. Label every number GAAP or non-GAAP.

## Sources
SEC EDGAR only (10-K, 10-Q, 8-K earnings releases, XBRL company facts), plus HubSpot
investor relations. Each figure needs its filing, period and a link.

## Output
- `memo.md`: a one-page summary of the business in numbers, with a table of every figure
  (value, period, definition, GAAP or non-GAAP, source link).
- Escalate if two filings disagree on the same figure, for example after a restatement.
