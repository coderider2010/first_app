# Module 01: HubSpot fact pack

**Engagement:** Project Hub · **Module:** 01-fact-pack · **From:** Associate · **To:** EM
**As of:** 2026-10-11 (latest reported quarter: Q2 2026, ended 2026-06-30) · **Confidence:** High

## Answer

HubSpot is a $3.45B-revenue (TTM to June 2026), ~98%-subscription software business growing ~20%. It is now
GAAP-profitable (3.8% TTM operating margin; 20.1% non-GAAP), converts ~19% of revenue to free cash
flow, holds $1.38B of cash and investments with no debt, and is shrinking its share count
through buybacks. Management guides FY2026 to $3.68B revenue (+18%), but its own guidance implies
growth slows to **~14% in H2 2026** (Q2 2026 was +20%).

**What this says (support for the answer):**
1. **Growth is decelerating.** Annual growth went 25% → 21% → 19%. Q1 2026 was +23.4% and Q2 2026
   +19.8%. FY2026 guidance implies Q3 of +14.2% and Q4 of +13.9% (as reported). Calculated billings grew only 14% in Q2 2026.
   Growth comes mainly from more customers (+14% YoY), not higher spend per customer: ASRPC rose 4%
   to $11,800, which the 10-Q attributes to Professional/Enterprise adoption and, in part, FX [S1].
2. **Margins are expanding at the operating line but compressing at the gross line.** Non-GAAP operating margin rose from
   15.5% to 20.3% in 2.5 years, and FY2026 guidance is 20.7–20.8%. GAAP gross margin fell from 85.0% (FY2024) to
   82.4% (Q2 2026); the non-GAAP subscription margin fell from 87.1% to 85.3% YoY. One fact behind this: amortization of capitalized software, which is
   booked in cost of revenue, rose to $39.7M from $29.3M YoY [S1]. *Hypothesis, not tested:* AI
   inference costs are a second driver.
3. **Free cash flow is real and growing.** TTM OCF is $856M. FCF is $658M (19.1% of revenue) after counting
   capitalized software as capex, the definition I recommend for the DCF. SBC ($515M TTM, 14.9% of
   revenue) is the main gap between GAAP and non-GAAP profit and is excluded from both FCF measures.
4. **Capital is being returned, not hoarded.** HubSpot bought back $500M of stock in FY2025 and $743M in H1 2026
   ($536M in Q2 alone). Shares outstanding fell from 52.7M (Feb 2026) to **49.87M (2026-07-31)**. $1.26B
   of authorization remains: $257.1M under the Feb 2026 program plus a new $1.0B program (Aug 2026). The 2025 convertible notes
   were settled in June 2025. A $500M secured revolver (Feb 2026) is undrawn. Debt is zero.

## Escalations to the EM (as the brief and charter require)

1. **Two filings disagree on FY2023 (and FY2022) profit: a restatement. Adjudicated; no action needed beyond awareness.**
   The FY2023 10-K and Feb 2024 release reported FY2023 gross profit of $1,824.7M, a GAAP operating loss of
   ($208.1M) and non-GAAP operating income of $330.3M [S7]. The FY2024 10-K [S6] revised these to
   **$1,831.9M, ($200.9M) and $337.4M**. The cause was an immaterial error in vendor contractual credits
   (cost of subscription revenue overstated by $7.1M in FY2023 and $6.2M in FY2022). Revenue and cash flow were
   unaffected. **I use the revised figures throughout.** XBRL flags these as `restated: true`.
2. **The NRR definition changed in 2025.** Under the new method, FY2024 NRR is 101.8% and FY2023 is 103.0%. The previous method gave 102.2% and 103.9% [S3].
   Use only the new-method series for trend analysis. NRR is disclosed annually in the 10-K only. No Q2
   2026 NRR appears in any SEC filing.
3. **Material events after the latest quarter that the brief did not ask about:**
   - **Restructuring (8-K, 2026-10-06) [S8]:** ~7% workforce reduction, with **$65–75M of charges** (mostly cash
     severance) mostly in Q4 2026. These charges are excluded from non-GAAP; FY2026 non-GAAP guidance was reaffirmed. GAAP FY2026
     operating income guidance ($197–201M) predates the plan and does *not* include these charges.
   - The 8-K references **longer-term operating margin targets from an Analyst Day on 2026-09-17**.
     These were not filed on EDGAR, so I have not collected them (they would be on the IR site).
   - The Chief Legal Officer is resigning, effective 2026-11-06 [S9]; no disagreement is cited.
4. **Relevant to the storyline (facts only, no view):** the working hypothesis turns on whether "fast
   growth plus margin expansion" is realistic. Management's own guide shows margin expansion continuing (FY2026 non-GAAP
   operating income guidance was raised from $736–740M to $762–766M) while revenue guidance was trimmed ($3.690–3.700B to $3.678–3.686B) and H2
   growth steps down to ~14%. Modules 2 and 3 should treat ~14% as the current run-rate exit growth.
