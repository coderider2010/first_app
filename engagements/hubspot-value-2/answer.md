# Project Hub II: the answer

**Status:** DRAFT, awaiting Partner approval (checkpoint 2) · **As of:** 2026-10-12 ·
**Price:** $228.30 (NYSE close, 2026-10-09)

## What is HubSpot worth per share?

> **About $145 to $330 per share, and the deciding question is whether HubSpot will ever
> turn revenue into cash the way mature software companies do. Today's $228 sits about 45%
> of the way from the pessimistic to the optimistic story: the market is close to a coin
> toss on it.**

| View | Method | Value per share | Module |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Squeezed:** AI pressure stalls the FCF-after-SBC margin at 12% and growth fades to 3% | DCF | **$145** | 03 |
| Peers, on cash flow (median 55.5x EV / FCF after SBC) | Trading multiples | $178 | 04 |
| *Squeezed growth with Grows-up margin (diagnostic, not a story)* | DCF | *$255* | 03 |
| **Today's price** | Market | **$228** | — |
| **Grows up:** margin rises to 25% (low-middle of mature peers), growth fades 13% → 4% | DCF | **$332** | 03 |
| Peers, on revenue (median 6.37x EV / revenue) | Trading multiples | $445 | 04 |

**Two independent methods agree on the hinge.** The DCF and the peer comparison were built
without seeing each other or the price analysis. Both locate the value on cash conversion:
the peer *cash* lens ($178) sits near Squeezed ($145), while the peer *revenue* lens ($445),
which implicitly credits HubSpot with peer-like cash economics, sits above Grows up. On
revenue HubSpot is the cheapest peer (3.07x vs 6.37x); on cash it is among the dearest
(73.6x vs 55.5x). The market is pricing it on cash.

*Not used:* Module 04's rule-of-40 regression value ($502), because growth plus margin
explained almost none of the peer multiples (r² 0.03).

## The assumptions that move the value most

1. **Long-run FCF margin after SBC.** Swapping only the margin path between the stories
   moves value by about $150 a share; swapping only the growth path, about $78 (Module 03).
   HubSpot converts 4.2% of revenue to FCF after SBC today; the seven-peer median is 14.9%
   (Module 04) and mature peers run 22.5–33.3% (Module 02). **The gap is not SBC**:
   HubSpot's SBC (14.9% of revenue) is slightly below the peer median (16.2%). It is cash
   generation before SBC: operating cash flow margin 24.8% vs 32.1%, and capex plus
   capitalized software 5.7% vs 2.3% of revenue (Module 04).
2. **The discount rate** (10.5%: 5.24% Treasury + 1.25 beta × 4.20% premium). ±1 point moves
   Grows up from $389 to $290 and Squeezed from $164 to $129 (Module 03); every Grows-up
   cell stays above the price and every Squeezed cell below it.
3. **The growth path.** The price accepts about 10% a year for ten years (Module 02),
   consistent with guidance fading from +14% in Q3 2026. Growth matters about half as much
   as margin.

## The working answer, tested

The working answer was: *"the price assumes HubSpot keeps out-growing mature peers while
expanding its margin toward theirs."* **It did not survive.** Module 02 found the price
needs only ~10% growth and a 15.6% long-run margin, below every mature peer (22.5–33.3%).
The Partner chose to proceed with 03 and 04 as the independent test rather than replicate
02; both point the same way. **The price assumes moderate growth and doubts the margin.**

## What would tell us which story is true

- **Q3 2026 results (early November):** net revenue retention (102% in Q2, down from
  103.5% in FY2025), net customer additions (~7,000 in Q2 vs 9,000–10,000 expected), and
  gross margin as AI credits are priced down (82.4% in Q2 vs 85.0% in FY2024).
- **Delivery of the 2027 target** of a 23–24% non-GAAP operating margin, and progress toward
  the Analyst Day 2030 targets (SBC at 10% of revenue).

## Caveats

- **Peer confidence is low:** the revenue and cash lenses differ by 2.5x, and the
  growth-adjusted fit failed. Excluding Twilio (a usage-based close call) lowers the cash
  lens from $178 to $158.
- **Valuation date:** both DCF modules discount from 30 Jun 2026, not today, understating
  value by about 3%; restructuring cash ($65–75M) is excluded, worth about $1.3–1.5 a share.
- **Share count** (52.40M diluted) has a ±1.3% range because unvested awards at 30 Jun are
  not disclosed; the Q3 10-Q will settle it, along with Q3 buybacks.
- **Unverified sources:** Analyst Day targets and quarterly NRR come from call transcripts
  (secondary sources). They support the stories but don't set a DCF number.
- **For the Partner (legal / accounting):** an immaterial FY2023 revision (revenue
  unaffected); the Chief Legal Officer resigned on 8 Oct 2026; a 7% workforce cut was
  announced on 1 Oct 2026.
