## Answer

HubSpot is a $3.45B-revenue subscription software business (TTM to Jun-26, GAAP), growing about 20% a year (Q2 2026 +19.8% as reported). It earns a GAAP operating margin of only 3.8% TTM but a non-GAAP operating margin of 20.1%, and the gap is mostly stock-based compensation ($515M TTM, 14.9% of revenue). Free cash flow is $658M TTM (19.1% of revenue). **After SBC it is $144M (4.2%)**, up from 1.6% in FY2025 and −8.4% in FY2023. The balance sheet holds **$1,383M of cash and investments and no debt**: the convertible notes matured in June 2025 and the new $500M revolver is undrawn. **Fully diluted shares are 52.40M at 30 Jun 2026** (49.66M basic plus 2.75M RSUs/PSUs; options are out of the money at $228.30), with a range of 51.33M–52.69M. Growth is slowing and margins are rising. Management guides Q3 2026 revenue to +14% as reported, cut FY2026 revenue guidance slightly in August while raising operating-income guidance, and on Oct 1 announced a 7% workforce reduction.

## Escalations to the EM

1. **Diluted shares need your sign-off (Medium confidence).** HubSpot does not disclose unvested RSUs/PSUs at 30 Jun 2026. The two nearest disclosures are 1.673M at 1 Apr 2026 (2026 proxy, point count) [S8] and 2.745M (10-Q Q2'26, *weighted-average* RSUs/PSUs excluded as anti-dilutive) [S3]. Under GAAP's treasury stock method nearly all of them were anti-dilutive: only 46k net dilutive equivalents of any type. I use **2.745M**, the later and more conservative figure, to give **52.403M**. It understates the 30 Jun balance if the grants came late in the quarter. Range: 51.33M (proxy count) to 52.69M (31 Jul basic of 49.866M [S3 cover] + 2.745M + up to 79k in-the-money options). The spread is ±1.3%, so per-share value moves by about 1%. The Q3 2026 10-Q (early November) or the FY2026 10-K will settle it. Recommend pinning 52.40M and noting the range.
2. **There is no convertible debt and no debt at all.** The brief expected convertible notes. The 2025 notes ($460M, 0.375%) matured and were settled in June 2025; 192k shares were issued on early conversions in Q1 2025 [S3]. No conversion price or maturity applies today. A $500M senior secured revolver (Feb 2026, Bank of America) is undrawn [S3].
3. **Material events after 30 Jun 2026 that the brief didn't ask about:**
   - **Buybacks.** A new $1.0B buyback was authorized on 3 Aug 2026, on top of $257.1M left on the February 2026 program [S3, S15]. Q3 buybacks will lower both shares and cash. Pinning both at 30 Jun keeps the bridge consistent: buybacks near market price are roughly value-neutral per share.
   - **Restructuring.** On 1 Oct 2026 HubSpot announced a 7% workforce cut, with $65–75M of mostly cash charges, mostly in Q4 2026. They are excluded from non-GAAP figures and guidance, and management cites "longer-term operating margin targets" from an Analyst Day on 17 Sep 2026 [S10].
   - **Management change.** The Chief Legal Officer resigned on 8 Oct 2026 [S11].
4. **Storyline signal for modules 02–04 (not against the working answer, but it sharpens it).** The business is already trading growth for margin. Revenue growth is guided down to about 14% for Q3, NRR is slipping, and margins and buybacks are rising. FCF after SBC is a thin 4.2% of revenue TTM, so the "margin expansion" branch starts from a low base. The stock is $228.30, against an average buyback price of $577.13 in Q2 2025 [S3].
5. **Restatement check (brief trigger): FY2023 was revised.** It is immaterial and resolved by the company, so there is nothing to adjudicate. In Q1 2024 HubSpot corrected an error in subscription cost of revenue (vendor credits). FY2023 gross profit went from $1,824.7M to $1,831.9M and operating loss from −$208.1M to −$200.9M; revenue was unaffected [S5, S1]. Per the conflict protocol I use the revised figures and record the originals in Exhibit A. No other figure in this pack differs between filings (XBRL "restated" flags checked for revenue, operating cash flow and SBC).
