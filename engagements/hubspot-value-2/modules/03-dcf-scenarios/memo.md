## Answer

**If HubSpot grows up like established software companies, it is worth about $332 a share. If AI pressure on per-seat software squeezes it, it is worth about $145.** Today's price of $228.30 (NYSE close, 9 Oct 2026) sits between the two, a little closer to Squeezed: it is 45% of the way from Squeezed to Grows up. The evidence does not pick either story whole. It splits by driver:
- **Margins** are maturing on schedule (favours Grows up). SBC is falling, the 2027 and 2030 margin targets went up, and costs are being cut.
- **Growth and pricing** look squeezed (favours Squeezed). Retention is down, customers are downgrading, AI agent prices were cut, and gross margin is eroding.

Crossing the two stories gives an estimate, not a third story: Squeezed's growth with Grows-up's margin is worth $255 a share, about 12% above the price. Margin is the bigger lever. Swapping only the margin path moves the value by about $150 a share; swapping only the growth path moves it by about $78. The fact that most decides which story is true is whether FCF after SBC reaches the high teens by 2029–30, as management's 2030 targets imply.

1. **Margin decides most of the value, and the margin evidence favours Grows up.** Under Grows up, FCF after SBC reaches 19% in year 5 (mid-2031). That is about a year behind what management's 2030 targets imply (estimate: 30% non-GAAP operating margin − 10% SBC − 1.0 pt TTM gap between FCF and operating margin ≈ 19%). The 25% end point is the low-middle of the pinned peer range (22.5–33.3%; peer median 24% with Intuit on its underlying 21.9%). Squeezed's 12% would mean management misses its 2030 target by about 7 points.
2. **Growth evidence favours Squeezed, but growth matters less.** Every growth signal is negative: decelerating guidance, falling NRR, downgrades, missed net adds, and AI price cuts. A bottom-up check supports a year-1 growth of 11–13% in both stories. Average customers +10.5% (H2 adds of 5,500 a quarter) and ASRPC +2% in constant currency give about 12.7%, against the guidance exit rate of about 14%. The paths agree within 10%.
3. **The squeeze has not shown up in seats.** The core mechanism of the Squeezed story is AI cutting the seats customers buy. That is not in the data yet: HubSpot seats are up 16% and Atlassian's seats are still expanding. What is visible is price deflation on the AI layer and budget-driven downgrades. Squeezed is a forecast about seats, not a description of them.
4. **The price implies a blend, and the blend the evidence supports is worth about $255.** At $228.30 the market EV is $10.58B: 3.1x TTM revenue, 16x TTM FCF, and 74x TTM FCF after SBC. Squeezed growth with Grows-up margins gives $254.60 (estimate, computed with the same model; not tuned, it simply crosses the two stories' stated drivers).

## What decides it (watch list)

1. **NRR and net upgrades: Q3 2026 results (early November) and FY2026.** NRR back above 103% with seat ARR still above 20% points to Grows up. NRR below 100%, or flat seats with credits failing to offset downgrades, points to Squeezed.
2. **Delivery on the 2027 margin: the 23–24% non-GAAP target, with SBC falling toward 12% of revenue.** Hitting it in FY2027 puts FCF after SBC on Grows up's path of about 13% by FY2028. A miss, or a reset of the 2030 targets, is the clearest Squeezed signal, because margin is the bigger lever.
3. **Gross margin under credit pricing.** If GAAP gross margin falls below about 80% while credit unit prices keep falling, AI is a cost the business cannot pass through, which is the core of Squeezed. If gross margin holds at 82% or above with credit consumption more than doubling, Grows up is more likely.
